DentAIstudy Partner Program Terms.

The terms governing participation, promotion, commission, qualification, and payouts.

These terms set out the conditions for participating in the DentAIstudy Partner Program and apply to every approved Partner.

1. Invite only access

DentAIstudy Partners is invite only. Access can be approved, paused, or removed by DentAIstudy in accordance with these terms.

2. Partner benefits

Accepted partners receive 3 months of Pro access, a partner code that gives their audience 25% off the first subscription payment, and access to a private dashboard. Pro access is extended to 12 months after qualification. Benefits remain subject to approval.

3. Qualification

Partners become qualified for cash payouts after 10 unique approved first subscription payments. Renewals do not increase this count. Refunded, charged back, disputed, fraudulent, self generated, or abusive payments do not count.

4. Commission

Commission starts with the first valid paid customer. Monthly plans earn 50% of the first payment and 20% of each of the next five successful renewals. Annual plans earn 30% of the first annual payment. Commission is calculated from DentAIstudy's recorded net earnings for each valid transaction after applicable taxes and payment processing fees. Refunded, charged back, and disputed payments are excluded.

5. Payouts

Payments have an approval period of up to 30 days. Qualified partners with at least $50 approved are paid between the 1st and 5th of each month through Wise.

6. Honest promotion

Partners must not make fake claims, guarantee exam results, or hide the partner relationship. Clear disclosure is expected.

7. Changes

DentAIstudy may update the program structure, discount rules, payout rules, or eligibility requirements as the program develops.
