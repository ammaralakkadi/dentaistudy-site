Partner Privacy

How partner records are handled.

This notice explains how DentAIstudy handles partner data.

This privacy notice is for DentAIstudy Partners. It covers partner-related records used to manage the program.

Information stored

DentAIstudy may store partner name, email, partner code, dashboard status, payment customer and transaction references, recorded net transaction earnings, confirmed customer counts, commission values, payout records, and activity logs.

Why it is used

Partner records are used to review partner performance, calculate approved commissions, manage payouts, answer support questions, and manage access.

Record review

Records are verified against our subscription information and internal account notes before dashboard or payout updates are confirmed.

Access

Partners should only see their own partner dashboard. Admin pages are for DentAIstudy internal use.

Contact

For partner privacy questions, contact partners@dentaistudy.com.
