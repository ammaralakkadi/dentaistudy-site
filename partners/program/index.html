Partner program

One clear place for the offer, commission, and payout rules.

DentAIstudy Partners is an invite-only program for selected dental creators, educators, and professionals with student audiences.

Who the program is for

The program is for people who already help dental students with clinics, vivas, OSCEs, boards, or subject exams. Partners should show genuine DentAIstudy workflows without exaggerated claims.

What partners receive

  • 3 months of DentAIstudy Pro after acceptance.
  • A private partner code with 25% off the first payment.
  • A dashboard based on verified customer, payment, and commission records.
  • Pro access extended to 12 months after qualification.

Core rules

  • Commission starts with your first valid paid customer.
  • 10 unique approved first payments qualify your account for cash payouts.
  • Payments enter an approval period of up to 30 days.
  • The minimum approved payout balance is $50.

Verified before approval

Customer payments, commissions, and payouts are verified before the partner dashboard is updated.

Step 1

Join and test

Accepted partners receive 3 months Pro access, a code, and a private dashboard.

Step 2

Earn from the first customer

Commission starts with your first valid paid customer; reaching 10 customers does not reset earlier commission.

Step 3

Qualify for payouts

At 10 confirmed unique paid customers, your account becomes qualified for cash payouts.

Commission & payouts

Rates that are simple to verify.

Commission is calculated from our recorded net earnings for each valid transaction. Refunded, charged-back, and disputed payments are excluded.

Monthly

50% + five renewals

50% of the first monthly payment, then 20% of each of the next five successful monthly renewals.

Annual

30% first payment

30% of the first annual subscription payment. Annual plans do not receive monthly renewal commission.

Payout

1st–5th monthly

Qualified partners with at least $50 approved are paid by Wise or bank transfer where available.

Attribution

The partner code must be entered at checkout.

Pending

The payment remains pending for verification for up to 30 days.

Qualification

Only unique approved first payments increase the customer count; renewals do not.

Approved

Valid commission remains accrued even before payout qualification.

Excluded payments

Refunds, chargebacks, disputes, fraud, and purchases made through your own Partner code do not qualify.

Not counted

Excluded records add neither customers nor commission.

Payout threshold

Your account is qualified and approved commission reaches $50.

Ready for payout

The balance enters the next monthly payout window.
